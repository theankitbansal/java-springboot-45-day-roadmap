## Assessment rubric

| Explanation | Cannot explain | Needs prompts | Explains independently |
| Coding/application | Cannot apply | Partial solution | Working solution with reasoning |
| Misconceptions | Core misconception remains | Some uncertainty | No important misconception observed |

A low score means review and retest; it is not a failure. Confidence is self-reported and does not replace assessment.
